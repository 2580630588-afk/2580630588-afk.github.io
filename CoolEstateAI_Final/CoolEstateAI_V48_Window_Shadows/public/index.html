AI and developer access

Our FastAPI service provides /v1/project, /v1/estates, /v1/scenarios/evaluate, /v1/buildings, and auto-generated /openapi.json and /docs.

Method and limitations

Outdoor risk and facade solar exposure are illustrative heuristic indices, not actual air temperature, UTCI, measured comfort, CFD, or validated energy predictions. Building footprints come from Buildings.sg where local data is supplied; geometry and heights may be incomplete.
