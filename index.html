Research / Environmental performance / 2025—Present

Wind–Solar–Thermal Performance Optimisation for a Natatorium Roof

A performance-led framework connecting architectural form, environmental simulation and data-driven optimisation.

Large-scale performance simulations were used to establish an extended dataset. Random Forest Regression and the NSGA-III algorithm then supported performance prediction and the generation of a Pareto solution set.
